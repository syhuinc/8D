Live 8D for other apps beta

Turn it on, then press play in your music app. With "Keep vocals in the center" on, the voice stays in the middle and the bass and highs move around you; turn it off to move everything. Lighter than converting a file. Uses your Orbit time and Effect strength below.

Off

Android will ask to let 8D Converter record audio. It only listens to your music app's sound to find the kick and bass hits; nothing is recorded or saved, and the microphone isn't used. Uses the Movement setting below. Some apps don't allow listening; then the log says so and the sound circles on its own.

Only if your music app never appears in the log below. Many phones ignore this (it did nothing on our test device): if nothing moves, turn it off.

What Live 8D sees

    Works on stereo songs. Other sounds mixed dead-center, often snare or lead guitar, stay in the middle with the voice.

    Fine-tune

    Instruments stuck in the middle with the voice? Go stricter. Voice drifting? Go looser.

    Only convert music you have the right to use. Sharing converted copies of copyrighted songs is still sharing copyrighted songs.
